01 · Discover

A city built in layers.

Chicago rewards the curious. Look up for bold architecture, follow the river through downtown, then keep moving until the skyline meets Lake Michigan.

02 · Highlights

Five Chicago frames.

Use the arrows, dots, or keyboard to move from polished landmarks to neighborhood streets.

Frame 01 · Downtown

Millennium Park

Public art, open space, and the mirrored curve of Cloud Gate place the city and its visitors in the same frame.

Frame 02 · River

Chicago Riverwalk

At river level, bridges and towers feel newly monumental while cafés, boats, and pedestrians keep the edge in motion.

Frame 03 · Lake

Lakefront

The horizon makes room to breathe. From the water, Chicago reads as one long architectural timeline.

Frame 04 · Transit

The Loop

Trains trace the downtown grid overhead, turning an everyday ride into one of Chicago’s most recognizable sounds.

Frame 05 · Northwest

Wicker Park

Murals, music, vintage storefronts, and busy sidewalks give this neighborhood a creative pulse of its own.
